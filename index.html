04 / Local by design

Your workspace is made from ordinary files.

Markdown stays Markdown. Images stay images. Documents stay in your folders.

iO ditor adds connections and tools around them without turning your work into a closed database.

Built around your files

File History

Review earlier versions of your documents, compare changes and restore a previous state without changing the way your workspace is organized.

Previous versions · Compare · Restore

MCP integration

Connect external AI and automation tools to the workspace through a controlled MCP interface, while keeping iO ditor’s files and project structure at the center.

External tools · Controlled access · Workspace context
